The idea at the heart of the project

The same One God.
Said in every tongue.

We say Hashem. We say Allah. We speak of the One in different languages and traditions. The One God Project begins with an invitation: what if we saw the same One behind those different words?

We don't have to agree on every belief, or pray in the same way, to make room for each other. These expressions come from different contexts. They sit side by side here, without making those traditions interchangeable.

"Even those who don't believe, don't believe in the same god as the believers..."A thought from the project's founder

And if you don't believe? There is room for you here, too. That thought is an invitation to a conversation, not a definition of anyone else's beliefs. Curiosity, kindness and a place beside each other are a beginning.

One finger,
pointed up.

A simple gesture for a shared idea: the God we call by different names is the same One. Across languages, countries and traditions, one finger points beyond our differences.

One humanity. Not one uniform way to believe.

The mission, written in 2016

“To introduce ONE God as the one infinite energy. This energy permeates the consciousness of all who exist and can be tapped into by collective experience.”

The best spiritual content, religious and otherwise, gathered from everywhere into one place, where open-minded discussion about spiritual matters can be shared without requiring any specific ritual.
